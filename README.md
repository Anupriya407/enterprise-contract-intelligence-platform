# Enterprise Contract Intelligence Platform (ECIP)

## Overview

Enterprise Contract Intelligence Platform (ECIP) is a long-term enterprise AI platform for managing, processing, and understanding contracts at scale.

The goal of ECIP is to provide organizations with a professional platform for document management, OCR, information extraction, contract intelligence, semantic search, knowledge graph exploration, and AI-assisted contract analysis.

---

## Project Vision

ECIP will evolve through five major versions:

- Version 1 — Enterprise Foundation & Document Management
- Version 2 — Contract Intelligence Engine
- Version 3 — Enterprise Knowledge Platform
- Version 4 — Enterprise AI Assistant
- Version 5 — Enterprise Contract Intelligence Platform

Version 1 focuses on building a production-ready backend foundation that future versions will extend.
